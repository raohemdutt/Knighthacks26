Index of every source cited in this folder's notes: `power-sampling.md` (PS-), `attribution-and-baselines.md` (AT-), `power-and-clock-levers.md` (LV-) and `request-windows-and-clocks.md` (WC-, added by telemetry refinement pass 1). Each row gives the source, its kind, its URL or DOI, the date it was opened, what part was read, and the findings that cite it. All sources were opened on 2026-10-10 by following the URL given; nothing was cited from memory.

Kinds: paper (peer-reviewed or preprint), vendor doc (documentation or driver source from the people who ship the tool), lab benchmark (benchmark suites, their data and methodology posts), issue (a GitHub issue thread), spec listing, review, our measurement (numbers measured on our own nodes; there is no ledger yet, so these point at the PR or task page that holds the number).

## Search log and gaps

The search ran on 2026-10-10 from about 6:40 to 7:20 AM ET with WebSearch, WebFetch, `gh` reads of GitHub and the Linux source tree, and a read of the ML.ENERGY leaderboard JSON (summarized with a throwaway script; nothing executed from the downloaded data). It stopped well inside the two-hour bound because the last three lookups added nothing new (later calls only re-checked author lists and one card's architecture): the AMD SMI overview blog repeated S01 to S03, a search for the meaning of tegrastats' average field found no documentation beyond S14, and a search for energy-per-token measurements on AMD Instinct GPUs with vLLM found only throughput and cost studies.

Open questions no source answered, each covered by a candidate experiment in the notes: the R9700's power-field averaging window and update period (PS-01, PS-05, PS-06); whether the AUP pods' driver and power firmware expose the RDNA4 energy counter (PS-03); any published joules-per-token measurement on an AMD GPU with vLLM (AT-14); the averaging window of tegrastats' second power value (PS-13); Orin Nano module power during llama.cpp decode for a 4B vision model (PS-20); and the CPU and power overhead of tegrastats or jtop on an Orin (PS-27).

Telemetry refinement pass 1 (2026-10-10, about 12:05 to 12:50 PM ET) added `request-windows-and-clocks.md` (WC-01, WC-02) and section 5 of `power-sampling.md` (PS-30 to PS-32), from sources S44 to S48 and a re-read of S10. Its search log is in `request-windows-and-clocks.md`, "Search log". Still open after it: the R9700's distinct-update period and averaging window for `SOCKET_POWER` (no source found; E6 on T0 measures the lag), the hub proxy's round-trip time, and the CPU cost of `tegrastats` at 100 ms on the Orin Nano (no source found).
